# KaamSaathi (काम साथी) — The Professional Network for Every Worker

> **“Every Skill Deserves an Opportunity.”**  
> *“Find trusted people. Find trusted work.”*

KaamSaathi is a premium, production-quality web platform that brings professional identity, trust, verified credentials, ratings, discoverability, and fair earnings to India's skilled trade and informal workforce (plumbers, electricians, mechanics, carpenters, AC technicians, cleaners, painters, and more).

Combining the best concepts of **LinkedIn + Urban Company + Rapido + Google Maps + a Dignity-First Reputation System**, KaamSaathi provides a dedicated digital ecosystem for both customers and skilled professionals.

---

## 🌟 Key Features

### 1. Dual-Role Experience (Customer & Worker Pro)
- **Customer Mode**:
  - Interactive hero search with location picker (Bengaluru, Mumbai, Delhi-NCR, Pune, Hyderabad, etc.).
  - 12 comprehensive trade service categories with pre-calibrated rate cards.
  - Interactive Worker Directory with Grid View and Live Map View.
  - Filter by verified credentials (PMKVY Govt Skill, Police Verified), availability, rating, and distance.
  - LinkedIn-style Worker Profile with verified certifications, trade test badges, community skill endorsements, before-and-after work photo gallery, and authentic customer reviews.
  - 4-step Booking Flow with voice note input simulation, photo attachments, and upfront price breakdown.
  - **Live Dispatch Tracker** (Rapido/Uber style): Animated map route, live countdown ETA, security OTP, technician call/chat, job progress stepper, UPI payment simulation, and review submission.

- **Worker Pro Mode**:
  - Online / Offline toggle with live radar status.
  - **Incoming Job Radar Simulation**: Real-time incoming job alert with audio chime, customer location, estimated earnings, and 1-click Accept / Pass.
  - Earnings Dashboard: Today's revenue, jobs completed, 5-star reputation score, and Withdrawable Wallet with Instant Payout to UPI.
  - Service radius slider (2–15 km) and custom labor price controls.
  - Commercial AMC contracts and project tenders feed.
  - Portable Digital Identity Card with QR Code and shareable handle (`kaamsaathi.in/@ramesh.electrician`).

### 2. Trust, Safety & Anti-Scam Protection
- **Anti-Scam Fair Price Calculator**: Side-by-side comparison between typical unverified market scam traps and KaamSaathi pre-calibrated benchmark prices.
- **Dignity of Work Charter**: Portable reputation, zero commission deductions on visiting fees, and bilateral mutual respect.

### 3. Tactile Polish & Accessibility
- **Apple-level Light Theme**: Warm off-white (`#FAF9F6`), soft ivory, deep charcoal typography, soft green trust indicators, and peach/orange accents.
- **Web Audio API Sound Effects**: Soft tactile clicks, incoming job alert chime, and booking completion fanfare.
- **Multilingual Support**: English, हिंदी (Hindi), and ಕನ್ನಡ (Kannada).
